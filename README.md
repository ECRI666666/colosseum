# Colosseum of Experience

**Summary:** A round-based co-op wave arena behind a sandstone portal. Earn points, unlock doors, buy gear and spend your rewards in the shop.

## What it is

Build a sandstone portal like a Nether portal and light it with flint and steel. It leads to a huge arena in its own dimension. Your normal inventory stays safe while you are inside.

- **Rounds:** Waves of zombies, skeletons, spiders and jockeys get harder every round. A boss with a health bar arrives every 10 rounds.
- **Points:** Kills earn points that you spend during the run on wall weapons, armor, a Mystery Box, perks and doors to new areas.
- **Perks:** Toughness, Swiftness, Might, Marksman, Fast Revive, Double Strike, Vampire and Second Chance. You can hold 4 at a time.
- **Arena Anvil:** Pick from 3 random enchantments for your weapon, across 3 upgrade tiers.
- **Decoy Creeper:** Throw a tiny creeper that lures nearby mobs and then explodes.
- **Co-op:** A fallen partner can be revived for 45 seconds by sneaking next to them.
- **Exfil:** After every 5th round you can call a hard exfil round for a 1.5x reward.
- **Points Shop:** Every round cleared pays out shop points. Spend them on XP, diamonds, netherite, enchanted books, an Elytra and more.

Available in English and German. The game language decides which one you see.

## Requirements

Minecraft 26.3 with NeoForge. Install the mod on both the server and every client.

## Building

You need Java 25.

```sh
./gradlew build       # the jar ends up in build/libs
./gradlew runServer   # dev server, runs the self-test
```
