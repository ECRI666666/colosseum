# Colosseum of Experience

**Summary:** A round-based co-op survival arena. Fight endless waves, earn points, unlock new areas and spend your rewards back home.

## Enter the arena

Build a sandstone portal the same way you build a Nether portal and light it with flint and steel. It takes you to a huge colosseum in its own dimension. Your inventory, XP and effects stay safe while you are inside, and they are waiting for you when you come back.

Pick a class, pick a difficulty, pull the lever and step through the gate. You start small, in a single courtyard, with a basic weapon and 4 steaks. The rest is up to you.

## How a run works

- **Endless rounds:** Zombies, skeletons, spiders and jockeys get tougher every round. Every 10th round brings the Arena Master, a boss with its own health bar.
- **Points:** Every kill and every cleared round earns points. Spend them on the spot on weapons, armor and potions from buttons on the walls.
- **Doors:** Pay to open iron gates into new areas: the North Court, the Pillar Hall and The Pit. The better gear is deeper in the map, and opening an area also opens its mob gates.
- **Power:** Find the switch in The Pit to turn on perks and the Arena Anvil.
- **Power-ups:** Double Points, One Hit Kill, Max Ammo and Nuke drop from kills.
- **Mystery Box:** Spend points on a random prize. It moves after a few uses.
- **Supply chests:** Loot chests appear every 3 rounds and after boss rounds.

## Perks

Hold up to 4 at a time. They are shown at the side of your screen, and you lose them when you go down.

| Perk | Effect |
|---|---|
| Toughness | +5 hearts |
| Swiftness | +20% run speed |
| Might | +3 damage |
| Marksman | +50% arrow damage |
| Fast Revive | Revive your partner in 1 second |
| Double Strike | +25% attack speed |
| Vampire | Every kill heals 1 heart |
| Second Chance | Survive one fatal hit, once per run |

## More tools

- **Arena Anvil:** Choose from 3 random enchantments for the weapon in your hand. There are 3 tiers per weapon, and after the last one every enchantment on it is maxed.
- **Decoy Creeper:** Throw a tiny creeper that lures nearby mobs, then explodes. It only hurts mobs.

## Better with friends

Play alone or with others. When you fall while a partner is alive, you stay down for 45 seconds. Your partner can revive you by sneaking next to you. If nobody does, you watch as a spectator and return next round without your gear.

After every 5th round you can call an **Exfil**: survive one extra hard round, then walk through the portal to leave with a 1.5x reward.

## Rewards

Gear from the arena stays in the arena. What you take home are **shop points**, and every cleared round pays more than the last one. Spend them at the Points Shop in the hall on XP bottles, diamonds, netherite, enchanted books, an Elytra, a beacon and more. Harder difficulties pay more.

A leaderboard in the hall shows the most rounds and the most points on your server.

## Good to know

- Requires Minecraft 26.3 and NeoForge. Install the mod on the server and on every client.
- Available in English and German, based on your game language.
- Blocks in the arena cannot be broken, and nothing you carry can get lost in there. Your own items are saved in your player data while you play.
- Server operators can open a test menu with `/colosseum debug`. Runs that use its combat cheats do not count for the leaderboard.
